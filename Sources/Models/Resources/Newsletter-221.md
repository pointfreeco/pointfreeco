For the past 8 years we have relied solely on GitHub for authentication on Point-Free. That has 
worked well because our audience consists only of developers, and every developer has a GitHub 
account. But over time we have received requests to support other forms of login, whether it be for 
a non-developer to administer the subscription, or a developer that is moving off of GitHub.

We are happy to say that we now support [passwordless login] with your email address. And if you 
sign up with just an email address, you can connect a GitHub account to your Point-Free account at 
any time from your [account settings](https://www.pointfree.co/account). There's even a little 
incentive to do so: connecting your GitHub account earns you a free episode credit that can be used 
on any subscriber-only episode.

If you've been holding off on [subscribing](https://www.pointfree.co/pricing) because you didn't 
want to tie your GitHub identity to your learning materials, or you just never had a GitHub account 
to begin with, there has never been a better time to [get started](https://www.pointfree.co/login). 
All you need is an email address.

[passwordless login]: /auth?kind=login
