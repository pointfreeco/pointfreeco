Our catalog has grown to 377 episodes and hundreds of hours of video, and till today the only way 
to find something in it was to remember which episode covered it. No longer: Point-Free now has 
[full-text search][search] across everything we have ever produced.

Every word of every transcript is indexed, from our dialogue to every line of code we write on
screen. Results show highlighted snippets of exactly where your search terms appear, and each
match deep-links to the precise moment in the episode, so you can jump straight from a search
result to the second we say it.

Search also understands a few tricks that are worth knowing:

  * Wrap words in quotes to match a phrase or code exactly, such as [`$0 + 1`][dollar] 
    or [`~Copyable`][copyable].
  * Combine terms to find episodes that cover all of them, even when they are discussed minutes 
    apart: [`parsing performance`][combined].
  * Use `OR` to match either term: [`wasm OR webassembly`][or-example].
  * Prefix a term with a minus to leave it out of results: [`uikit -swiftui`][exclusion].
  * Capitalize a type name to prioritize case-sensitive matches: [`Never`][never] finds the type, 
    not the adverb.

And you can refine any search further by sorting by relevance or date, restricting matches to 
code, prose or titles, and filtering to free or subscriber-only videos.

Search is available to everyone, right now, at [pointfree.co/search][search]. If there's an API, 
concept, or snippet of code you remember us covering, it's now only a search away. And if 
searching reveals just how much we have covered over the years, there has never been a better time 
to [subscribe](/pricing) and watch it all.
