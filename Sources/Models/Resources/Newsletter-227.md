Just a quick reminder for [Point-Free Max][pricing] members: our first [Office Hours][office-hours]
session is coming up soon, and we would love to hear what you want us to cover.

Office Hours are smaller, more casual livestreams where we answer questions from Max members. Every
session is recorded, and the archive will be available from the [office hours page][office-hours]
afterward.

## Ask and vote

We built a dedicated Q&A system for Office Hours so that you can shape what we discuss before the
stream begins. Head to the [Open questions tab][office-hours-open-questions] to:

* **Submit a question** for us to answer live. Markdown is supported, and questions are anonymous.
* **Vote on questions** from other members so we know what topics are most useful to cover.

After each session, answered questions will link directly to the timestamp in the recording where
we discussed them.

See you soon!
